Calibration

Sets up the handle for your unit. You only need this once per enclosure, or after moving the magnet. Click Start, then just follow the prompts: each step records by itself once you're holding still. Sound on helps.

  1. Rest — hands off the handle
    not recorded
  2. Push up all the way and hold
    not recorded
  3. Push down all the way and hold
    not recorded
  4. Push left all the way and hold
    not recorded
  5. Push right all the way and hold
    not recorded

Fine-tune: trace a square

Evens out the speed in each direction after calibration. Using mousetrap, move the cursor onto the green dot, then trace the square clockwise, once round. Push with the same comfortable pressure on every side and don't correct for speed: that's what it measures. Your curve and line straightening are switched off during the test and put back after.
